Code of Conduct

Our Commitment
We want to create a respectful, friendly, and inclusive environment where everyone feels welcome and comfortable sharing their ideas. We value different backgrounds, experiences, and opinions.

Expected Behaviour
All team members and contributors should:
Treat everyone with respect and kindness.
Listen to others and respect different opinions.
Communicate clearly and politely.
Give everyone an equal opportunity to participate.
Provide constructive feedback and support each other.
Respect everyone's time and contributions.

Unacceptable Behaviour
The following behaviours are not acceptable:
Discrimination, harassment, bullying, or personal attacks.
Offensive or disrespectful comments.
Excluding others from discussions or project activities.

Consequences
If someone violates this Code of Conduct, the team will discuss the issue and ask them to stop the behaviour. If the behaviour continues, the person will receive a warning. Repeated or serious incidents may be reported to the DSCI 522 instructional team.

Reporting Procedure
Anyone who experiences or witnesses unacceptable behaviour can report it privately by contacting the team at maryan.hosseini@gmail.com. The team will take the concern seriously, respect everyone's privacy, and try to resolve the issue fairly.
If the issue cannot be resolved within the team, it will be reported to the DSCI 522 instructional team.

Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related activities, including team meetings, GitHub discussions, pull requests, and online communication.
